# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The staff of the Fonds de Développement de l'Artisanat (FDA, Bénin), working at a desk on a computer:

- the secretary records the funding requests sent by artisans, associations and NGOs, corrects them, then hands them to verification;
- the verifiers read each request, suspend it with a reason or approve it with an amount, then record the post-support report (people trained, by sex and disability);
- the directors follow the pipeline, read the statistics and close the fiscal year.

Each screen serves one step of that workflow. The phone is rare; the desktop is the reference.

## Product Purpose

Track a funding request from its submission to the end-of-allocation report, and give management reliable figures: amounts requested and granted, beneficiaries by sector, sex and disability. Success is a request processed without hesitation about its state, and figures a director can quote without checking them again.

## Brand Personality

Serious, sober, trustworthy. A public institution's working tool: the figures and decisions come first, decoration does not. Its identity follows the official site fda.bj (forest green, olive, sage, Manrope), with the restraint expected of an internal tool rather than the editorial flourish of the public site.

## Anti-references

- The generic admin template the app uses today: pastel icon cards, giant numbers everywhere, a colored header band with nothing in it.
- Too colorful or festive: a different color per chart, gradients, gradient borders, decorative animation.
- Shadows and visual effects everywhere, and pages that load several CSS frameworks and scripts for nothing.
- Austere and dated: grey on grey, small text, 2000s administrative forms.
- A foreign "startup" look unrelated to a Beninese public institution.

## Design Principles

1. **The workflow is the structure.** Navigation, labels and counts follow the steps of a request (en attente, transmise, suspendue, approuvée, rapport déposé, clôturée), with the same words on every screen.
2. **Figures are the content.** Amounts and headcounts are aligned, formatted the same way everywhere (FCFA, French dates) and readable at a glance; nothing competes with them.
3. **One action per screen is obvious.** The next step of the request is the one visible primary action; the rest is secondary.
4. **Earned restraint.** Every color, border or shadow must carry information. Default to flat, light and fast.
5. **Same identity as fda.bj.** Staff should feel they are inside the FDA, not inside a template.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast at least 4.5:1, visible keyboard focus, states never conveyed by color alone, French throughout, desktop first with usable small screens.
